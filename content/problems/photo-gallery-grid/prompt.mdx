Lay out a photo gallery with **CSS Grid**: three equal columns, even gaps, and one featured photo that takes up two columns. Only edit `styles.css`.

## Requirements

- `.gallery`:
  - `display` is `grid`.
  - Three **equal** columns: `grid-template-columns` is `repeat(3, 1fr)` (writing `1fr 1fr 1fr` also works).
  - `gap` is `16px`.
- `.featured` (one of the photos): spans **two columns**, with `grid-column` set to `span 2`.
- `.gallery img`: `width` is `100%`, so each photo fills its cell.

## Hints

- `fr` means "a fraction of the leftover space", so `1fr 1fr 1fr` makes three equal columns.
- You never need to define the rows: the grid adds them as the photos fill the columns.

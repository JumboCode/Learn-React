Write a short "About Me" page using headings and paragraphs.

## Requirements

- One top-level heading (`<h1>`) with the text `About Me`.
- At least **two** paragraphs (`<p>`) under it. Write anything you like about yourself.
- A second-level heading (`<h2>`) with the text `Hobbies`, after the paragraphs.
- At least one paragraph after the `Hobbies` heading.

Keep every element properly closed, and put everything inside `<body>`.

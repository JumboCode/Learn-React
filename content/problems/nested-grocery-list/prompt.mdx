Write a grocery list where one of the items has its own list of choices inside it.

## Requirements

- A top-level heading (`<h1>`) with the text `Grocery List`.
- An unordered list (`<ul>`) with exactly three items, in this order: `Fruit`, `Bread`, and `Milk`.
- Inside the `Fruit` item, a **nested** unordered list with two items: `Apples` and `Bananas`.

## Hints

- A `<ul>` should only have `<li>` elements as its direct children.
- To nest a list, put the inner `<ul>` **inside** the `<li>` it belongs to, right after the word `Fruit`, and before that `<li>` closes.

The Jumbo Bakery home page was built entirely out of `<div>` elements. It looks fine, but screen readers cannot tell which part is which. Rebuild it with semantic landmark elements.

## Requirements

- A `<header>` at the top of the page containing the heading (`<h1>`) `Jumbo Bakery` and a `<nav>`.
- The `<nav>` contains two links: `Menu` (to `#menu`) and `Hours` (to `#hours`).
- A `<main>` after the header that holds the page content, including the `Today's Menu` and `Hours` sections.
- A `<footer>` after `<main>` with the text `Jumbo Bakery, 123 College Ave.`
- Do not put the header or footer inside `<main>`.

## Hints

- Swap each `<div>` for the element that describes it. Most of the content can stay exactly as it is.
- Keep the `id` attributes so the nav links still work.

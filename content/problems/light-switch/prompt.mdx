Build a light switch: one button that flips a light on and off.

## Requirements

- When the page loads, a paragraph says `The light is off.` and the button's text is **Turn on**.
- Clicking **Turn on** changes the paragraph to `The light is on.` and the button's text to **Turn off**.
- Clicking **Turn off** switches back. It keeps flipping on every click.

## Hints

- Store a boolean in state: `const [isOn, setIsOn] = useState(false);`
- `!isOn` is the opposite of `isOn`.
- Use ternaries to choose the paragraph text and the button text from `isOn`.

Build a short-message box that counts characters as you type, like a social media post form.

## Requirements

- A `<textarea>` labelled `Message`.
- A paragraph that shows the current length out of 50, updating on every keystroke: `0 / 50 characters` at first, then `5 / 50 characters` after typing `hello`, and so on.
- When the message is longer than 50 characters, also show a paragraph `Too long by N characters` (for example `Too long by 3 characters` at 53). Hide it again once the message is 50 or shorter.
- A **Post** button that is **disabled** when the message is empty or longer than 50 characters, and enabled otherwise.
- Clicking **Post** shows a paragraph `Posted: ` followed by the message, for example `Posted: hello`.

## Hints

- Use `onChange` on the textarea and read `e.target.value` in the handler.
- Keep the text in state; the length, the warning, and `disabled` can all be computed from it during render.
- Pass the handler to `onClick`, do not call it: `onClick={handlePost}`, not `onClick={handlePost()}`.
